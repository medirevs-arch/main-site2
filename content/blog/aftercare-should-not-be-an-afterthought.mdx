> Discharge isn't the finish line. Aftercare is where recovery actually
> happens, and it's still one of healthcare's biggest blind spots.

Treatment often ends at discharge. Recovery does not. The period after someone
leaves a facility is where a course of medication is finished or abandoned,
where a follow-up appointment is kept or missed, and where a complication is
either caught early or arrives back at the hospital as an emergency.

It is also the part of the patient journey with the least infrastructure
behind it. A visit is recorded. A discharge is recorded. What happens over the
following weeks usually is not — which means nobody is in a position to notice
when it goes wrong.

At Medirevs we treat that gap as a design problem rather than a compliance
problem. Structured reminders, follow-up that is scheduled rather than hoped
for, and a record that stays with the patient after they leave the building.

Healthcare shouldn't stop at the hospital door.
