> We're closing in on testing and launch. Follow us to be the first to know
> when it goes live. 🚀

DoctoRevs is entering testing. The thing we most want to get right before it
goes live is the part that usually gets dropped: what happens between visits.

An appointment ending is not the same as a condition being managed. A chronic
condition needs attention more often than once a month, and the check-in that
catches a problem early is rarely the one that was formally scheduled.

That is the behaviour we are testing for now — not just whether a consultation
works, but whether the follow-up around it holds up on a real network, on a
mid-range phone, in a clinic that is busy.

More soon.
