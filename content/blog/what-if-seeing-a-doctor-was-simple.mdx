> What if seeing a doctor didn't require hours of travel, waiting, and
> paperwork?

With Medirevs Telemedicine, patients can book appointments, consult with
doctors online, receive prescriptions, request laboratory tests, and access
follow-up care — all from a connected digital platform.

Listed out like that it sounds like five features. It is really one idea: that
those five steps are a single visit, and that treating them as one thing is
what removes the travel, the waiting and the paperwork between them.

Today a single visit usually involves four different people and at least as
many handovers. You see a doctor. A prescription goes to a pharmacy. A sample
goes to a lab. Right now those handovers happen on paper, over the phone, or
not at all — and the record ends up in pieces.

Healthcare should be accessible, convenient, and continuous.

Because better healthcare starts with better access.
